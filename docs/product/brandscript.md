# Brandscript — Nuvo through the StoryBrand (SB7) frame

**Status:** canonical (v1, 2026-07-25) · owner: Phil · revisit when positioning shifts
**Frame:** Donald Miller's *Building a StoryBrand* — SB7. A brand is not a hero. **The
customer is the hero; the brand is the guide.**

This is not only marketing copy. It's a **product decision tool** — see §9, the four tests.
Every feature either helps the hero move through this story, or it's noise.

---

## The one-liner

> **You run more than one world. Nuvo is the one funnel from what you're responsible for,
> down to the hour on your calendar — so you can end a week knowing what actually moved.**

Short forms:
- **7 words:** *One funnel, from your commitments to your calendar.*
- **Tagline:** *Know your week is the right week.*
- **Category line:** *A daily planner with a conscience.*

---

## 1 · A Character — who wants what

**The character:** an operator carrying **several worlds at once** — paid work with teams
and a roadmap, a community that depends on them, a discipline that needs protected hours,
and people at home whose calendar does not care about any sprint. *(The five domain kinds
in [`personas.md`](./personas.md) §1. Names vary per operator; the shape doesn't.)*

**What they want (external):** to end each week knowing the things that matter actually
moved — and to have a plan for next week they believe in.

**What they want (deeper):** to be *faithful* in what they're responsible for. Not more
productive. Faithful. They don't want to squeeze more in; they want to stop losing the
important thing to the urgent one.

> Character detail lives in [`personas.md`](./personas.md). Every persona is one variation
> of this same want.

## 2 · Has a Problem

**The Villain — Drift.**

> *Drift* is the slow, blameless gap between what you say matters and what your calendar
> actually spends you on. It never announces itself. Nothing goes wrong on any given day.
> Then it's March and the project that mattered in January hasn't been touched, and you
> can't point to a single week where you decided to abandon it.

Drift is the right villain because it is:
- **Singular** — one named enemy, not a list of annoyances.
- **Relatable** — everyone with more than one responsibility feels it.
- **Root-cause** — it explains the tool sprawl, the guilt, and the surprise deadline.
- **Not a person** — we never make the user the villain. They didn't fail; the system had
  no gate.

Every level of the problem:

| Level | The problem |
|---|---|
| **External** (what's tangible) | Work lives in a board, meetings live in a calendar, personal things live in a list, and the half-shaped idea lives in a note. Nothing reconciles. There's no place that can answer *"can I actually carry this week?"* |
| **Internal** (how it feels) | *"I was busy all week and I can't tell you if anything that matters moved."* Low-grade guilt about the untouched project. Dread that something is about to blow up that you can't see yet. The specific shame of the domain you keep starving — usually family. |
| **Philosophical** (why it's wrong) | Time is the one thing you are given and cannot get back. **The things you've been made responsible for deserve better than to be lost between apps.** A week is a stewardship, not a queue. |

## 3 · Meets a Guide

A guide needs two things: **empathy** and **authority.**

**Empathy** — *"We know what it's like to hold several worlds and feel like you're failing
at most of them. We didn't build a productivity app. We built the thing we needed on a
Sunday night."* Nuvo is built by one of its own operators, for a real week with real
obligations — not designed from a market study.

*Note the register: **"one of its operators," not "for me."** Built-by-a-user is authority;
built-for-one-user reads as a hobby. Same fact, and the second framing loses the room.*

**Authority** — earned, concrete, never boastful:
- It holds **every calendar you actually own** — Google (two-way, with its own mirror
  calendar), Microsoft 365, Apple/iCloud, ICS — on one surface.
- It carries the **whole vertical**, from a domain you've committed to down to a 40-minute
  block on Tuesday. Nothing else does both ends.
- It answers *"can I carry this week?"* with **arithmetic, not vibes** — demand ÷ capacity,
  calibrated against your own proven pace from completed blocks.
- The Review closes the week with **evidence**, not self-report.

**Voice rules for the guide:** calm, plain, specific. Short sentences. Concrete verbs. The
guide never panics, never hypes, never scolds. It says *"here's what's true,"* then waits.

## 4 · Who gives them a Plan

**The process plan — three steps, always these three:**

1. **Capture everything.** One key, free text. `call David tomorrow 9am 30m #church !high`
   becomes structure. Nothing lives in your head or in five apps.
2. **Commit a week you can actually carry.** Sunday pulls from your bets, checks it against
   your proven pace, and composes a week around the meetings you can't move. You accept it.
3. **Land it on the hour — and let Friday tell you the truth.** Drag it onto the day. The
   Review harvests what moved and carries the rest forward, so Sunday never starts cold.

**The agreement plan — what we promise:**

- **You stay in charge.** Nuvo proposes into quiet pools. Nothing reaches your calendar
  without you moving it.
- **We won't nag you.** No streaks, no red badges of shame, no debt ledger.
- **We'll tell you the truth**, including when the truth is *"you can't carry this week."*
- **Your account is yours alone.** Nobody else is in your funnel — no manager's dashboard,
  no shared board, no one watching your calendar. *Say this out loud: it's a real
  differentiator that we currently never state (ledger row O4).*
- **It works on your phone**, because your week doesn't wait for you to open a laptop.

## 5 · And calls them to Action

- **Direct CTA:** **Request access** → then *Open app*. (One primary action per surface.)
- **Transitional CTA:** the honest, low-commitment thing that demonstrates the guide's
  competence before asking for anything. Candidates, in preference order: *"The Sunday
  Question"* (a one-page walkthrough of composing a week you can carry) · a short
  *Commitment* explainer (demand ÷ capacity, why your week feels impossible) · a Warm Paper
  screenshot tour of Schedule + Domain.

*Currently the site has the direct CTA only. Adding one transitional CTA is the single
highest-leverage marketing gap — see [`roadmap.md`](./roadmap.md).*

## 6 · That helps them avoid Failure

What's at stake if nothing changes — state it soberly, once, never as fear-mongering:

- Another quarter where the important thing was always *next* quarter.
- A calendar full of other people's priorities and none of your own.
- The surprise deadline you should have seen three weeks out.
- The domain that quietly went dark — and the conversation at home that follows.
- Being *excellent* at the urgent and *absent* from the significant.

## 7 · And ends in Success

- **Sunday you know.** The week is decided, and the arithmetic says you can carry it.
- **Tuesday you're in it.** The block is on the hour, and the prep is already written.
- **Friday you can prove it.** The Review shows what moved — with evidence, not memory.
- **Every domain stays lit.** Nothing goes dark for a quarter without you choosing it.
- **The quiet one:** you stop carrying the whole system in your head.

## 8 · Identity transformation

| From | To |
|---|---|
| Reactive operator | **Faithful steward** |
| Busy | Deliberate |
| "I think I'm on track" | "I can show you what moved" |
| Guilty about the untouched project | Honest about what you chose not to do |
| Holding it all in your head | Holding one funnel that holds it for you |

> The aspirational identity is **steward**, not *optimizer*. This is why we don't do
> streaks, leaderboards, or "productivity scores" — they serve the wrong identity.

## 9 · How the brandscript governs the product

Run any idea through four tests before designing it:

1. **The Villain test.** *Does this reduce Drift?* If a feature doesn't shorten the gap
   between what matters and what gets time, it's a convenience — fine, but name it as one
   and rank it accordingly.
2. **The Hero test.** *Does this make the user more capable, or the app more impressive?*
   Anything that makes Nuvo the hero (auto-scheduling that owns your day, an assistant that
   acts unasked) is a story error, not just a taste error.
3. **The Plan test.** *Which of the three steps does this serve — capture, commit, or land?*
   If it serves none, it's a fourth step, and a fourth step is a tax on the hero.
4. **The Failure test.** *Which specific failure does this prevent?* If you can't name one
   from §6, the feature has no stakes and will feel like clutter.

## 10 · Language canon

**Say:** funnel · the Week · commit · carry · land it on the hour · domain · faithful ·
gain · what moved · steward · propose · promote · quiet · evidence · honest.

**Never say:** *crush it · hustle · 10x · productivity hacks · supercharge · effortless ·
AI-powered · let AI run your day · unlock · game-changer · seamless.*

**Retired forever** (see [`decisions.md`](./decisions.md) D-006): *harvest · tend · nurture
· cultivate* and any farming or pastoral metaphor. Also avoid *grind*, and avoid *ritual*
in user-facing copy where *flow* or the ceremony's own name will do.

### The register — explicit out, tangential in (D-027)

Nuvo is built on Christian convictions about time, responsibility, and doing work well.
**Those convictions drive the product; they are not a prerequisite for using it.** The
excellence is the witness — the copy doesn't have to be. So:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **Explicit — out** | Presumes the reader shares the faith, or names it | *called · calling · what God has given you · ministry* (as a default domain) · scripture · any devotional register |
| **Tangential — in** | Carries moral weight, fully usable by anyone | **steward · faithful · vow · gain · discipline · integrity · presence · showing up** |

The test: *would a reader who shares none of these convictions still find this the most
precise word — or would they feel addressed as an outsider?* "Steward" passes; "called"
doesn't. When a tangential word is doing work no synonym does, **keep it** — the answer to
"is this too much?" is precision, not dilution.

Applied: Domain is now *"where you've committed to show up"*, not *"where you are called to
be faithful."* Same idea, no entry fee.

**Watch:** "Nuvo" is both the app and the assistant. In copy, say **"ask Nuvo"** for the
assistant and **"in Nuvo"** for the app; never let a sentence need both.
